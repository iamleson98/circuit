AM-RX — the receiver as a chain of rescues
Each stage saves the signal from one problem until audio comes out of the speaker
FROM AIR
Antenna
1-2 m wire
Passes through the room's radio waves and picks up a blur of every station at once (µV-mV)
everything, faintly
CHOOSE ONE
LC tank
L1 ∥ CV1 · TUNE
A coil and the tuning cap resonate: only the selected station survives; everything else is shorted to ground
one station
DON'T LOAD IT
Buffer
Q1 · follower
Copies the tank voltage without draining it, keeping the tuning sharp (Q stays high)
same, sturdier
LOUDER
RF amplifier
Q2 · gain ≈ 90×
Lifts the millivolts of radio into tens/hundreds of mV — enough for a diode to bite
big RF
UN-WRAP
Detector
D1 (1N34A) + C8
Keeps only the tops of the RF cycles → what's left IS the voice that rode the envelope
audio again
TO YOUR EAR
Audio amp
RV1 + LM386
Volume knob, then up to 0.5 W into the speaker (GAIN switch: 20×/200× for weak stations)
Side branch: the detector's DC level also feeds Q3 + LED2 — the "signal LED" that glows when you are tuned into a strong station, which turns finding the transmitter into a visual game.
