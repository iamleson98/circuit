AM Station
A tunable AM transmitter and its matching receiver — four transistors and a coil on one board, three transistors and a diode on the other. Every stage is derived from first principles, proven by simulation, and buildable on a perfboard with parts from any hobby shop. You will hear your own voice come out of a radio you tuned yourself.
