Why both caps to GND?
The crystal is a lossy series R-L-C in parallel with C0. In the Pierce oscillator (inside the MCU), C1 and C2 form the feedback divider with the inverting amplifier. Equal caps give symmetric drive; the series pair appears to the crystal as ONE capacitor of half the value — that is the load it must "see" to oscillate at exactly 16.000MHz.
