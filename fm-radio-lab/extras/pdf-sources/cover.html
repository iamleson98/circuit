FM Station
A tunable FM transmitter and its matching super-regenerative receiver — two transistors on one board, four on the other, and a hand-wound coil they share. The transmitter's own junction capacitance performs the modulation; the receiver IS the transmitter, strangled 50,000 times a second. Every stage is proven by simulation before you solder it.
